# Core Business Requirements
* We are building Voice assistants that help the customer success team reduce manual calls and issues resolving.

# Problem Statement:
* When a customer want to have any help from the customer success team, earlier they used send mails to C.S team and get the both of the parties availablities and schedule meetings or calls.
* This is time consuming and inefficient. So we want to have voice assistant that can help us to resolve the issues of the customer directly.
* Yeah obviously the issues can take less time when a human interaction is done, but with the help of AI agents we can resolve the issues of the customer directly and efficiently.
* So we are going with the solution of Voice Assistant named "Madhu".

# Madhu : Voice Assitant
## Capabilities:
* Madhu is a female voice assitant and she can speak in multiple languages. But mainly concentrated on English, Telugu, Hindi.
* She can able to understand the customer queries and respond to them appropriately.
* If incase of unsolvable query, Madhu will connect to live agent who is available to pick up the calls from clients/customers.
